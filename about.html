About Dok's Off Sales

Committed to quality products, excellent service, and supporting our local community.

Our Story

Dok's Off Sales is a proudly local liquor store located in Khayelitsha, Western Cape. We are dedicated to providing our customers with a wide range of beers, whisky, cognac, spirits and energy drinks at competitive prices. Our commitment is built on honesty, quality service, and creating a welcoming shopping experience for everyone.
